# Robinhood — Q2 re-asked in 2022 after market shift

Robinhood's original Q1/Q2 resolution (2013-2014) was clean: democratize investing for amateur retail investors who couldn't access commission-free trading. The thesis powered growth through 2020. By 2022, the market context had shifted dramatically — the meme-stock cycle had peaked and ebbed, interest rates were rising, and the amateur-investor cohort's engagement had decayed.

The Q2 status was no longer resolved. Robinhood ran Q2 again with a new cohort hypothesis: **active traders, not amateur investors**, were the cohort with rabid post-2022 demand. The signal pattern shifted from "everyone trades a little" (amateur, Hard Fact-style accepted-friction reframe) to "this specific cohort trades a lot, frequently, with sophisticated needs" (active trader, closer to Hair on Fire urgency).

The re-evaluation is the lesson, not the specific cohort. Q2 is not resolved-once-and-done. When the market context shifts, the cohort with rabid demand shifts. The Sequoia framework explicitly notes (article 2, verbatim) that "real practice is messier than theory" and "strictly linear" progression isn't expected.

## What the founder did

- Recognized the cohort decay in engagement and retention data.
- Re-ran Q2 evidence gathering with the new cohort hypothesis.
- Identified active traders as the rabid-demand cohort post-shift.
- Re-tuned product, pricing, and positioning for the active-trader cohort.

## What they refused

- Refused to keep optimizing for the 2013-era "democratize investing" ICP despite brand momentum.
- Refused to claim Q2 was still resolved when the cohort signal had decayed.

## How this applies to your situation

If you're at Q2 and you "had PMF" but lately something feels off, Robinhood is the case to match against. The lesson: Q2 can regress. A market shift, a cohort decay, or a competitive entry can move you back from Q2-resolved to Q2-unresolved. The honest move is to re-run Q2 with a new cohort hypothesis, not to defend the old resolution with diminishing data.

The archetype-re-evaluation is the harder move. The 2013-era Robinhood was Hard Fact (Hard Fact reframe of "trading is inaccessible to amateurs"). The 2022+ Robinhood is closer to Hair on Fire for active traders (clear, urgent need for low-fee, fast-execution platforms). Same company, different archetype, different operating priorities.
